# ❤️ Happy 1st Anniversary Website (25 November Special)

A romantic, interactive, and heartwarming web application built with **Next.js**, **React**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **HTML5 Canvas**, crafted specially to celebrate your 1st Anniversary on **25 November**!

---

## 🌹 What's Inside The Website

1. **✨ Romantic Hero & Live Love Clock**:
   - Live counting clock: Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds together since 25 November 2025.
   - One-click name personalization ("Happy 1st Anniversary, [Her Name]! ❤️").
   - Dreamy floating heart particles and ambient glows.

2. **🎵 Floating Romantic Music Player & Melody**:
   - Interactive vinyl record player widget that spins with a warm synthesized music box melody (Web Audio API).
   - "Our Song?" upload button allowing you to load your special couple song (MP3) or audio.

3. **🌟 365 Days of Milestones (Love Roadmap)**:
   - Glowing central ribbon timeline with cards:
     - Day 1: The Day Our Universe Changed (25 Nov 2025)
     - First Date: Coffee That Went Cold & Endless Laughs
     - Pure Magic: The First Time I Said "I Love You"
     - First Trip: Our First Little Getaway & Adventure
     - Everyday Magic: The Sweet Ordinary Days
     - 365 Days!: 1 Year Down, Forever To Go (25 Nov 2026)
   - "Add Our Own Milestone" button to record custom private memories.

4. **📸 Polaroid Memory Wall & Photo Gallery**:
   - Authentic Polaroid styling with washi tape, tilt angles, and romantic handwritten captions.
   - Filter tabs: All, Dates, Adventures, Cozy Moments, Favorites.
   - Click to zoom into full-screen romantic lightbox.
   - Heart reaction counter on each photo.
   - Built-in **"Add Our Real Photos"** feature with drag-and-drop / file selector that saves directly to local storage!

5. **🎮 Adorable Mini-Game: "Catch My Love!"**:
   - Control a romantic Cupid basket with mouse, arrow keys, or finger touch.
   - Catch falling hearts (+10), love letters (+25), roses (+35), and stars (+50).
   - Unlocks tiered anniversary rewards:
     - 100 points: Secret Love Note
     - 200 points: Lifetime Unlimited Hugs Pass
     - 350 points: Romantic Love Coupons!

6. **🎟️ 6 Redeemable Love Coupons**:
   - 30-Min Relaxing Massage Pass
   - Dinner Date Anywhere You Want
   - Movie Night Choice Pass (No complaining!)
   - Midnight Ice Cream / Dessert Run
   - Win Any Argument Ticket (Instant surrender)
   - One Universal Free Wish
   - Real "Redeem Now ❤️" button with stamp and date tracking!

7. **💌 Wax-Sealed Secret Love Letter**:
   - Vintage envelope sealed with a heart wax stamp.
   - Tap to break seal with sound effect, unfold the parchment, and read the romantic letter.
   - "Edit Letter" button to customize the words with your private jokes and memories.

8. **🍯 "Why I Love You" Honey Jar**:
   - Interactive jar with 25+ deeply touching reasons.
   - Shake to draw random sweet reasons.

9. **🧠 Couple Trivia Quiz**:
   - Cute 4-question soulmate quiz with humorous and sweet commentary.

## 🚀 How to Run The Website

### Option 1: Quick 1-Click Launch (Recommended)
Simply double-click the `run.bat` file located inside:
`C:\Users\karan\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\anniversary-website\run.bat`

This will automatically start the lightweight local server and open `http://localhost:3000` in your default browser.

### Option 2: Run via Terminal (Next.js Dev Server)
```bash
cd "C:\Users\karan\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\anniversary-website"
npm run dev
```
Then open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.

## 🎁 How to Personalize for Your Girlfriend

1. **Names**: Click the **"Customize Names"** button below the main title on the homepage to set her name/nickname and your name.
2. **Photos**: Click **"Add Our Real Photos"** in the Polaroid section to upload pictures from your phone or laptop.
3. **Love Letter**: Click **"💌 Open My Love Letter"**, break the wax seal, and click **"Edit Letter"** to personalize your heartfelt message.
4. **Music**: In the bottom-right corner, click **"Our Song?"** to upload the MP3 of a song that is special to you both.
